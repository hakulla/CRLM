# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want CRLM to be a respectful, useful project for everyone who participates. Contributors,
maintainers, and users are expected to treat one another with professionalism and good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give technical feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, ask clarifying questions, and accept correction.
- Keep discussions relevant and free of harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's credentials, personal data, private messages, or
  device information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting malware, destructive code, credential-stealing code, or code the
  contributor does not have the right to license.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, releases, and other
spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (the repository owner
profile) or through the repository's Security tab when confidentiality is important. Do not post
personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly as possible. The maintainer may remove content, request an
apology, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Retaliation is not tolerated.
